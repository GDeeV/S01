## Warning signs
Early signs are shivering, clumsiness, fumbling hands, and poor judgment. As it worsens, a person becomes confused, slurs words, and grows drowsy. When shivering stops in a cold person, hypothermia is severe.

## Causes
Hypothermia can happen well above freezing, especially with wind, rain, wet clothing, or immersion in cold water. Wet clothing loses most of its insulating value.

## Warming a person
Get the person out of wind and rain. Replace wet clothing with dry layers. Insulate them from the ground. Warm the center of the body first: chest, neck, head, and groin, using blankets, a sleeping bag, or skin-to-skin contact. Give warm sweet drinks only if they are fully alert and can swallow.

## Severe hypothermia
Handle the person gently and keep them lying flat. Rough movement can trigger dangerous heart rhythms. Get emergency help.
