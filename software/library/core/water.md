## Why treat water
Clear-looking water from streams, lakes, and springs can still carry germs such as Giardia and Cryptosporidium. Treat all surface water before drinking it.

## Boiling
Boiling is the surest way to kill germs. Bring clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute. At elevations above 6,500 feet (about 2,000 meters), boil for 3 minutes. Let it cool before drinking.

## Cloudy water
If water is cloudy, let it settle, then pour it through a clean cloth, paper towel, or coffee filter before boiling or disinfecting.

## Disinfecting with bleach
If you can't boil water, use unscented household bleach. For 1 gallon of clear water, add 8 drops of 6% bleach or 6 drops of 8.25% bleach. Double the amount for cloudy water. Stir and wait 30 minutes. The water should smell slightly of chlorine; if not, repeat the dose and wait another 15 minutes.

## Filters
Portable filters with a pore size of 1 micron or smaller remove Giardia and Cryptosporidium, but most filters do not remove viruses. Combine filtering with boiling or disinfection when in doubt.
