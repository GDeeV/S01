## Identifying it
Pacific poison oak grows as a shrub or a climbing vine. Leaves grow in groups of three, with lobed or scalloped edges, and can be shiny or dull. They turn red in late summer and fall. In winter the plant may be bare, but the stems still carry the irritating oil.

## After contact
Wash skin with soap and cool water as soon as possible. The oil sticks to clothing, tools, and dogs' fur, so wash those too.

## Never burn it
Smoke from burning poison oak carries the oil and can cause severe reactions in the lungs.
