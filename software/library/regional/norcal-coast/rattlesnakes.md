## The local species
The northern Pacific rattlesnake is the only venomous snake native to this region. It has a triangular head, a rattle on its tail, and blotched brown or gray markings.

## When and where
Rattlesnakes are most active from spring through fall. They rest on warm trails and rocks, and shelter under logs, rocks, and brush.

## Avoiding bites
Watch where you put your hands and feet. Step onto logs and rocks rather than over them. Give any snake you see a wide berth and let it move away. Most bites happen when people try to handle or kill a snake.
