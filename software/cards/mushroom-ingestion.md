title: Ate a wild mushroom or plant
order: 5
source: Based on poison control guidance (paraphrased sample)
vetted: false
---
1. Get to emergency care now, even if the person feels fine. Deadly mushroom symptoms can take 6 to 24 hours to appear.
2. Bring a sample of what was eaten, wrapped in paper, not plastic.
3. Note what time it was eaten and how much.
4. In the US, Poison Help is 1-800-222-1222 if you can reach a phone.
- Wait for symptoms before getting help
- Trust a feeling of recovery after early symptoms
