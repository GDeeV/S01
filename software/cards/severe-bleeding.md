title: Severe bleeding
order: 1
source: Based on Stop the Bleed guidance (paraphrased sample)
vetted: false
---
1. Make sure the area is safe. Use gloves or a plastic bag as a barrier if you have one.
2. Press hard directly on the wound with cloth or gauze. Keep pressing without letting up.
3. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top. Do not remove the first layer.
4. For life-threatening bleeding from an arm or leg that pressure doesn't stop, apply a tourniquet 2 to 3 inches above the wound, not on a joint. Tighten until the bleeding stops. Note the time.
5. Keep the person lying down and warm. Get help.
- Remove a tourniquet once it's on
- Lift the dressing to check the wound
