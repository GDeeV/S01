source: Based on wilderness first aid guidance (paraphrased sample)

---
1. Move away from the snake. Do not try to catch or kill it.
2. Keep the person calm and as still as possible.
3. Remove rings, watches, and tight clothing near the bite before swelling starts.
4. Keep the bitten limb still and at roughly heart level.
5. Get to a hospital. Antivenom is the treatment. If alone, walk out slowly and steadily.
- Use a tourniquet
- Cut the wound or try to suck out venom
- Apply ice or drink alcohol
