title: Hypothermia
order: 3
source: Based on NPS and CDC cold-stress guidance (paraphrased sample)
vetted: false
---
1. Get the person out of wind, rain, and snow.
2. Replace wet clothing with dry layers. Insulate them from the ground.
3. Warm the chest, neck, head, and groin first with blankets or body heat.
4. Give warm sweet drinks only if they are fully alert.
5. If shivering has stopped or they are confused, handle them gently, keep them flat, and get help.
- Rub their arms and legs
- Give alcohol
