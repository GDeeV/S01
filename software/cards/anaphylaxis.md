title: Severe allergic reaction
order: 4
source: Based on anaphylaxis first aid guidance (paraphrased sample)
vetted: false
---
1. If the person has an epinephrine auto-injector, use it now in the outer thigh. It can go through clothing.
2. Have them lie down with legs raised. If breathing is hard, let them sit up.
3. Get emergency help.
4. If there is no improvement after 5 to 15 minutes and a second auto-injector is available, use it.
- Wait to see if symptoms get better on their own
